Delete your Mixaloon data

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Mixaloon keeps a copy of your game progress in the cloud, so you can get it back on a new phone. You can delete it, and the account it belongs to, at any time.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Mixaloon and tap the gear (Settings).
  2. Under Your progress, tap Delete my data.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

This deletes your progress in the cloud and on this phone, and removes your sign-in. If you used Save with Google, the game may ask you to sign in with Google once more so the sign-in can be removed; if you cancel, your progress is still deleted, and you can email us to remove the sign-in. If you never used Save with Google, an empty sign-in record (an ID with no game data and nothing about you) can remain. Your settings (such as sound and color symbols) stay on the phone. It can’t be undone. You need an internet connection; if there is none, nothing is deleted and you can try again later.

Without the game

If you can’t open the game, email mixaloon.support@fremtech.se with the subject “Delete my Mixaloon data”.

  • If you used Save with Google, write from that Google account’s email address (or tell us which address it is). We use it only to find your account.
  • If you never used Save with Google, your account is anonymous: we can’t tell which save is yours from an email. Use Delete my data in the game instead, or uninstall the game; a cloud save nobody uses holds only game progress (levels, stars, coins and similar) and nothing about who you are.

We delete your cloud save and your sign-in within one month and reply when it is done.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Your cloud save (levels, stars, coins, boosters, lives, places, hats): deleted.
  • Your code redemption records (your account ID and the time, one per code you used): deleted with Delete my data; by email, we delete them by hand.
  • Your sign-in (the account ID, and your Google email and name if you linked Google): removed. If you never linked Google, an empty sign-in record with no game data can remain.
  • An earlier anonymous save left behind when Save with Google moved you to an existing save, and its code records: these hold only game progress and are not reached by Delete my data. We may delete them in clean-ups, and delete them when you ask by email.
  • Your progress on the phone: deleted when you use Delete my data in the game; uninstalling the game also removes it.
  • Statistics and crash reports: not linked to your account. They are deleted automatically after their retention period (2 months for statistics, 90 days for crash reports).
  • Your email to us: kept 12 months after the conversation ends, then deleted.
