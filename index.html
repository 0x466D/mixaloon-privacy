Mixaloon

Pop balloons. Mix paint. Paint the town.

Mixaloon is a puzzle game for Android: pop groups of red, yellow and blue balloons, blend the paint they leave into orange, green and purple, and color a little town one level at a time.

The game is in a closed test on Google Play.
