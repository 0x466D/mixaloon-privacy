Privacy policy

This policy explains how the game Mixaloon (“the game”, “we”) handles information. The short answer: the game does not collect, send or share any personal data.

Who is responsible

The game is made by {{DEVELOPER}}, {{TOWN}}, Sweden. Contact for anything about privacy: mixaloon.support@fremtech.se.

What the game stores, and where

The game saves your progress and settings only on your phone, in the game’s own private storage: which levels you have won, your stars, lives, coins and boosters, and your settings (such as color symbols, sound, music and vibration). This never leaves your phone through the game. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Your phone’s own backup (Android backup to your Google account) may include this game data, if backup is turned on on your phone. That backup is managed by Google under your own account; we have no access to it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. It works fully offline.
  • It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting service and no in-app purchases. In this test version the shop items and the offers are free.
  • It has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • It does not use your advertising ID.

Sharing your painting

After a win you can share a picture of your painting, if your phone supports it. The picture goes only where you choose in your phone’s share menu; we don’t receive it.

Permissions

  • Vibration, so pops can be felt (you can turn vibration off in Settings).
  • Internet is listed because it is part of the app framework the game is built with. The game does not use it.

Error log

The game may keep a small log of technical errors on your phone, to help fix bugs. It stays on your phone. It is only sent to us if you choose to copy it and send it yourself, for example in an email.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use it only to answer you. We delete support emails 12 months after the conversation ends. Our email is handled by {{EMAIL_PROVIDER}}.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the game does not collect personal data, we hold no data about you, except emails you send us. Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you can ask us to see, correct or delete those emails: write to mixaloon.support@fremtech.se. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY), www.imy.se.

Changes

If a future version of the game adds anything that collects data (for example ads or crash reports), we will update this policy before that version is released, and change the date at the top.
